# Council Company OS — v4 product contract

## Company #001

**Council Labs** runs Council itself as its first company.

The founder supplies direction and retains consequential judgement. The system should absorb coordination, research routing, task formation, memory and status compression without hiding disagreements or creating unreviewed external side effects.

## Core objects

- **Company** — mission, founder focus, principles and identity
- **Agents** — role, department, operating prompt, model/tool configuration and authority level
- **Projects** — owner, goal, health, progress, milestone and project room
- **Rooms** — project-specific conversation, durable agent memory and mission queue
- **Decisions** — questions that genuinely require founder judgement, with prepared options and agent recommendations
- **Company memory** — mission, principles, decisions and lessons that persist beyond a chat
- **Activity** — a lightweight audit trail of meaningful internal changes

## Authority ladder

1. **Observe** — analyse and comment only
2. **Recommend** — propose plans and internal work
3. **Act with approval** — prepare actions, but human approval gates consequential side effects
4. **Autonomous** — execute explicitly pre-approved low-risk workflows once the required tools and auditability exist

In v4, the interface models all four levels, but external side effects remain human-approved by default.

## Company cycle

A company cycle serialises the current founder focus, project state, open founder decisions and internal missions into the HQ room. The Chief of Staff is directly invited and can summon the specialists needed to challenge the state and create next actions.

The cycle should answer three things:

1. What is actually true now?
2. What is the highest-leverage unresolved tension?
3. What should happen next, and does the founder need to decide anything?

## Success criterion for v4

Council v4 succeeds when the founder can open the product and understand the state of the company before opening a chat — and when a company cycle produces better priorities and concrete work from real company state rather than generic brainstorming.
